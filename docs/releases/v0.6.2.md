## Second Nature 0.6.2: Field Crew refinement

**Stable Factorio 2.0.77 with Space Age, Quality and Elevated Rails. Alpha prerelease.**

Download `second-nature_0.6.2.zip`, keep it zipped in the Factorio `mods` folder, and replace older Second Nature copies after backing up saves. Use the named mod ZIP, not GitHub's source archive. A matching SHA-256 file is attached. Private repository downloads require GitHub sign-in.

### Keep the existing drones, make activation clear

The inventory-based wind-up drones and field controller are retained as requested. **No armor, equipment grid, batteries or power supply are needed.** After Field construction robotics (20 red science after Automation), carry the controller, drones and materials in the character inventory.

**New crews are enabled automatically. Ctrl + Shift + B pauses/resumes them and opens the monitor.** The toolbar drone button is the same control. A one-time notice explains this when the kit is detected. Previously saved deliberate pauses are preserved; press the key to resume those crews. Hiding the monitor does not pause work.

### Planner support and tuning

Native blueprint/book, copy/cut/paste, undo/redo, import, deconstruction and upgrade tools unlock with early field robotics. Drones build ghosts, dismantle explicitly marked entities and return their real contents, or apply eligible upgrades while consuming new items and returning old ones. Native configuration and quality are preserved, with accounting for connected underground pairs.

- Base: 18-tile radius, 2.1 tiles/s, 1.5-second work cycle.
- Tuning 1: 40 red science; 22 tiles, 2.52 tiles/s, 1.25-second work cycle.
- Tuning 2: 60 red + green; 26 tiles, 3 tiles/s, 1-second work cycle.
- Capacity remains 64 by default, configurable to 128 per player, with a 512-drone server bound.

The crew does not join logistic networks, dock in roboports, perform logistic requests, repair or supply requested modules. Packed vehicle construction/upgrades, perishable place-items and specialized rail upgrades remain outside this barebones system. Loose items below a ghost must be cleared or explicitly marked for deconstruction. Real cargo reservations, cancellation, permissions and inventory-overflow safeguards remain in place.

### Smooth 3D-rendered drones and explorer refinements

Drone positions now update every tick, and their original model has sixteen directional views, a raised canopy, layered casing and articulated gripper. Ground shadows remain separate from airborne bodies. These are pre-rendered 3D sprites within Factorio's 2D graphics, not a real-time 3D conversion.

The explorer's chest is now a smaller, tapered teardrop profile with fuller lower volume rather than oversized spheres. Boots have a rounded heel/toe outline and curved toe cap. The right palm grips at **48% of the shaft**, near its midpoint, above the left palm at 16%. The weighted swing and actual gameplay mining speed are retained.

### Verification and limits

**235 source/offline tests** cover the existing campaign plus startup, controls, planner gates, material/quality accounting, deconstruction contents, upgrades, pair accounting, tuning, smooth directional movement and the revised mesh/grip contracts.

[The official-engine validation passed](https://github.com/Radukan/FC-Test/actions/runs/34166208791): save creation/reload, in-flight construction, and real native mining/upgrades on a loaded chest, a rare inserter with custom vectors and an underground pair. Release publishing revalidates the exact tagged source and re-downloads the uploaded ZIP/checksum for verification.

No graphical-client hotkey/appearance test, exhaustive third-party-mod test, complete campaign balance pass or long-run GPU/UPS/multiplayer profile is claimed. Previews are source-art studies, not gameplay footage.
