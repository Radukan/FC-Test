## Second Nature 0.6.1: Field Crew

**A small-version update for stable Factorio 2.0.77 with Space Age, Quality and Elevated Rails. Alpha prerelease.**

Download **`second-nature_0.6.1.zip`**, keep it zipped, and put it directly in the Factorio `mods` folder. Back up saves and replace older Second Nature copies first. Use the named mod ZIP, not GitHub's automatic source archive. Private repository downloads require GitHub sign-in with repository access. A matching `.zip.sha256` file is attached.

### Early construction assistants

Research **Field construction robotics** after **Automation**, for only **20 automation science packs**. Craft a field controller and wind-up drones from iron, gears, circuits and copper cable. Carry them and your construction materials, then press **Ctrl + Shift + B** or the drone shortcut.

- **64 concurrent drones by default**, configurable up to **128 per player**; 512 active drones server-wide as a safety bound.
- **18-tile radius**, **2.1 tiles/second** flight and **1.5 seconds** of construction work after arrival.
- Build entity and tile ghosts using actual matching-quality items reserved from the character inventory.
- No batteries, armor grid, roboports, logistic cells, chest access or delivery requests. Native roboport robot inventories reject these drones.
- Construction only: no repair, deconstruction, upgrades or requested-module delivery. Specialized packed vehicles and loose ground items are left alone.
- Cancellation, failed work, full inventories, owner transitions and save/reload reconcile reservations. Destroyed drones are lost, but their unspent cargo can be recovered.
- Separate airborne body and ground-shadow animations, with a small enable/pause/recall monitor.

### More human movement and mining

The explorer's pelvis and shoulders counter-rotate, weight shifts through the stance, the head has independent bob and stabilization, and relaxed arms swing opposite the advancing leg. Foot swing velocity is continuous at lift-off and contact.

Mining has an anticipation, a faster downstroke, body drive, a brief weighted impact and recovery. The **right hand leads closer to the pickaxe head along the shaft**, with both palms attached throughout the cycle. A stable hand basis fixes the previous degenerate orientation near horizontal swings. Native particles align with the contact frame.

The adult explorer remains fully clothed in protective equipment. The suit's chest silhouette is fuller and rounder, with bounded secondary motion that is reduced by heavier armor. Native aim directions, frame counts, movement speed and actual mining productivity remain unchanged.

### Compatibility and verification

The 0.6.0 expanded plants, Wayfarer lander, music, jukebox, defenses and precision logistics remain included. Existing factories, cargo, research and ecological values are not reset, and existing saves are not granted free drones.

**223 source/offline tests** cover the existing campaign plus the new inventory, quality, cancellation, crew-size, permissions, lifecycle, sprite and motion contracts. The required stable-engine scenario saves with drones in flight, reloads, constructs real normal/rare entities and a tile, checks exact material conservation across two operators, and verifies isolation from a nearby powered roboport.

The publisher revalidates the exact tagged source and re-downloads the uploaded ZIP/checksum for byte comparison and SHA-256 verification before publication is considered complete.

Headless does not certify native GUI interaction, every character/Mech transition, subjective graphical appearance, audible playback, GPU/UPS performance or a long-run multiplayer/desync test. Source previews are not gameplay footage. See `docs/FIELD-CREW.md`, the verification ledger and agent report for details.
