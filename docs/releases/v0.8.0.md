## Second Nature 0.8.0: Nightglass Foundry

**Stable Factorio 2.0.77 with Space Age, Quality and Elevated Rails. Alpha prerelease.**

Download **`second-nature_0.8.0.zip`**, keep it zipped in the Factorio `mods` folder, and replace older Second Nature copies after backing up saves. Use the named mod ZIP, not GitHub's automatic source archive. A matching SHA-256 file is attached. Private repository downloads require GitHub sign-in.

### Startup and field crew corrections

- **Iron sticks are available from the start**, including after updating existing forces. Wood-free power poles no longer wait for green science.
- The drone control is now an **upper-left mod button**, visible only while the character carries a field controller. It is no longer a toolbar shortcut. **Ctrl + Shift + B** remains available.
- Pausing recalls workers visibly. They fly back with their real cargo and return to inventory at arrival, not through instant remote deletion/refund. Re-enabling preserves those return flights.
- Death, logout and incompatible surface/force transitions still reconcile safely when physical return is not possible.

### Humanized explorer and distinct icons

The original adult goth explorer has a sculpted, smoothly shaded face and skin, slimmer arms, continuous shoulders, less bulky protective gear, a more open face and corset lacing instead of a mechanical abdominal grille. Morticia Addams' adult-goth elegance is a loose styling reference; no photographic likeness is copied. The wolf cut, tattoos, covered short skirt, rounded boots and native motion/grip contracts remain.

**117 item/building icons** are individually authored objects with stronger contrast and more distinct silhouettes, reviewed at both 64 px and 32 px. Armor icons show armor; inserters show their arms; glass, minerals, carbon, membranes, depleted components and fluids no longer reuse nearly identical family drawings. Factorio-style scale and a restrained industrial palette are retained.

### Six different power systems in each stage

| Early | Mid | Late |
|---|---|---|
| Photovoltaic bank, 120 kW | Biomass engine, 3 MW | Photonic canopy, 12 MW |
| Burner alternator, 600 kW | Geothermal wellfield, up to 8 MW | Planetary thermal tap, up to 30 MW |
| Wind turbine, up to 400 kW | Residue cogenerator, 12 MW | Combined-cycle gas, 60 MW |
| River paddle, 350 kW | Heliostat tower, 1.8 MW | Solid-oxide biogas cells, 24 MW |
| Twin-cylinder steam, 1.8 MW | Biogas turbine, 6 MW | Load-following fission, 80 MW thermal |
| Producer-gas alternator, 1.2 MW | High-pressure steam recovery, 18 MW | Magnetoplasma conversion, 150 MW |

These are **18 systems total**, not six overall. Ratings remain bound by sunlight, wind, shoreline, fuel, supplied heat/plasma, fluid flow, residue handling or construction cost. Geothermal clusters share regional output budgets; surviving 0.7 wells retain their former baseline. Reactor ratings are thermal and need heat exchangers/turbines. Plasma conversion requires a real fusion supply and hot-coolant handling. Existing occupied plant footprints are not enlarged.

See `docs/POWER-ROSTER.md` for costs, energy ratios, geography and scaling examples. The solar-only railway and all earlier restoration, drone/planner, defense, logistics and audio content remain.
