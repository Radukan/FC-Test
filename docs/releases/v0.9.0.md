## Second Nature 0.9.0: Restoration Record

**Stable Factorio 2.0.77 with Space Age, Quality and Elevated Rails. Alpha prerelease.**

Download **`second-nature_0.9.0.zip`**, keep it zipped in the Factorio `mods` folder, and replace older Second Nature copies after backing up saves. Use the named mod ZIP, not GitHub's automatic source archive. A matching SHA-256 file is attached. Private repository downloads require GitHub sign-in.

**Existing saves upgrade in place.** No recipe, research cost, power rating or ecological rate changed in this release, so an in-progress campaign keeps its exact numbers. Milestones your force has already deserved are awarded the first time the updated save runs.

### Nineteen restoration milestones

The achievement window now records the restoration. Twelve medallions are tracked by the mod, because the engine knows nothing about succession stages, captured spores or a five-world network hold:

**First breath** (stage 1), **Root systems** (stage 3), **A living world** (stage 5 with clean air and a matured landscape), **New growth** (1,000 established trees), **Terraformer** (10,000 reclaimed tiles), **Deep green** (50,000 captured pollution or spores), **Signal restored** (first rocket from the wreck camp), **Common ground** and **Quiet eden** (the two Nauvis decisions), **Hold the line** (survive a response wave with the target intact), **Field stations** (work recorded on all five worlds), and **Second nature** (the complete ten-minute network hold).

Seven more are native condition prototypes that the game evaluates on its own: **First gust**, **Network nodes**, **Coordination cells**, **Matrix gardener**, **Read the world**, **No shortcuts** and **Nightglass grid**, the last requiring 100 GJ inside an hour with no emitting generator on the grid.

These are modded achievements. Factorio keeps them in its separate modded list, so nothing here touches vanilla or Steam progress.

Awards belong to a **force**, not to whoever happened to be online. A planetary medal goes to every force with recorded restoration cycles on that world, a player who was offline receives it on joining, and merging forces keeps the earlier award time without double-awarding. A **Milestones** tab in the field station (**Shift + T**) lists the tracked medallions with their goals, greys out locked ones and shows how long ago each was earned.

### An in-game field guide

Eleven entries were added to the native tips-and-tricks window, in their own **Second Nature** category. No stock Factorio tip is replaced, reordered or hidden.

They cover the restoration briefing, starting from stone instead of wood, your first restoration cycle, byproducts as real inventory, the hotspot survey gate, the two kinds of native pressure, circuit telemetry, the eighteen power systems, the solar railway, field crews without a robot network, and finishing the network. Entries offer themselves when they become relevant and stay quiet when a player has clearly already learned the lesson.

### The factory has a voice

In 0.8.0, **sixteen of the eighteen generating plants ran silently**. All of them now have a working sound chosen by mechanism, so plants that work the same way sound the same way: photovoltaic, wind, hydro, combustion, heavy engine, steam, turbine, gas turbine, deep earth, electro and telemetry layers. The ecology circuit monitor ticks like the instrument it is.

Generating loops scale with real activity. A becalmed wind turbine, a solar bank at midnight and a starved gas turbine go quiet instead of droning at full volume.

**95 carried items** were missing inventory handling audio and now have move, pick and drop sounds matched to their material family: minerals rattle, glass and cultures click, composites thud, barrels ring, armor and weapons use their own stock sets.

Every referenced sound is a stock base or Space Age asset. Nothing is copied into this mod, and a test verifies that each path is genuinely used by an upstream prototype in the pinned 2.0.77 data, so a misspelled filename cannot ship.

### Verification and limits

**281 local source tests pass**, 24 of them new: milestone declaration and uniqueness, the script-versus-engine type split, reachability of every scripted award, prototype and icon existence, condition targets resolving to real prototypes, medallion distinctness, per-force isolation, offline joins, force merges, the dashboard tab, tip-category isolation, the absence of any silent plant or silent item, and the bundled README's rewritten release links. Two native markers were added, `SECOND_NATURE_ENGINE_MILESTONES_OK` and `SECOND_NATURE_ENGINE_AUDIO_OK`, running against a real force and real loaded prototypes.

Not claimed: the on-screen look of the modded achievement window, notification timing in a graphical client, mixing levels judged by ear, tip pacing across a full playthrough, and behavior alongside third-party achievement or sound-replacement mods. See the verification ledger and [MILESTONES.md](https://github.com/Radukan/FC-Test/blob/v0.9.0-factorio-2.0/docs/MILESTONES.md) for the complete evidence and reporting limits.
