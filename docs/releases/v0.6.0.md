## Second Nature 0.6.0: Verdant Works

**Stable Factorio 2.0.77 with Space Age, Quality and Elevated Rails. Alpha prerelease.**

### Install

Download **`second-nature_0.6.0.zip`**, keep it zipped, and put it in your Factorio `mods` folder. Back up saves and replace older Second Nature copies first. Use the named mod ZIP, not GitHub's automatic source archive. This private repository requires GitHub sign-in with repository access.

The matching `.zip.sha256` file contains the archive checksum. For the full arrival sequence and starter camp, begin fresh Space Age freeplay on Nauvis with the default mod settings.

### Articulated explorer

- Constant-length two-bone legs with bending knees, planted stance, lifted swing, toe roll and weight transfer.
- Detailed protective equipment, articulated elbows, visible gloved palms, four segmented fingers and an opposing thumb.
- Sixteen-frame normal and armed locomotion, preserving the native paired aim/stride layout and three armor appearances.
- A twenty-frame mining cycle at 0.26 authored animation speed, about 28% shorter than the previous visual cycle. The pickaxe point leads the strike; actual mining productivity is unchanged.

### Distinct, larger process plants

Fermentation trains, greenhouse terraces, membrane banks, clarifiers, furnace drums, cyclone filters and habitat courts give the process families different silhouettes. Weathered steel, copper service loops, solar shades and contained vegetation provide a restrained industrial-solarpunk treatment.

Complex new construction uses **5 x 5** footprints, with cryogenic gardens, sanctuaries and planetary beacons at **7 x 7**. Simple stations remain compact. Existing compact machines retain their original geometry and pipe positions; factories are never silently expanded or relocated on load. Mining a compact machine returns the normal item, whose next ordinary placement uses the larger plant.

Rendering includes complete building shadows in every rotation. Compensated sprite shifts preserve native ground pivots and pipe seams. Recipes, crafting speed, energy requirements and ecological rates are retained.

### Wayfarer landing shuttle

The lander has a tapered riveted hull, sloping cockpit glazing, swept shoulders, twin atmospheric engine pods, cold exhaust bells, lift fans, stabilizers, hydraulic landing struts and a cargo ramp. Folded solar cells and sealed seed canisters carry the restoration theme.

The hull and ground shadow are drawn once, with a small opaque overlay for animated standby systems. Existing camps are refitted by replacing only their render object. The original collision box, 48 cargo slots, position, remaining supplies, rocket history and permanent protection are preserved. Updates do not replenish cargo or recreate missing ships.

### Existing content retained

The voiced arrival, We Need a Living World, After the Ash, jukebox, biological defenses, inserter endpoint editor and endgame logistics remain included. The anthem retains its previously authored **spoken** lyrics; it is not a sung-metal performance.

Controls: **Shift + T** field station, **Ctrl + Shift + P** local smog, **Shift + I** inserter vectors, **Ctrl + Shift + J** nearby jukebox.

### Verification and limits

**192 source/offline tests** pass, alongside the official stable-engine create/reload and 2,100-tick probes. Coverage includes expanded layouts, pipe connectivity, native inserter vectors, campaign APIs and a render-only lander refit with deliberately depleted cargo. Sprite checks cover complete framing, visible animation, exact ship-overlay reconstruction and current preview fingerprints.

The release publisher revalidates this exact tagged source, builds the installable archive, uploads the ZIP/checksum and re-downloads both for byte comparison and SHA-256 verification. Assets appear after the required jobs finish successfully.

Headless checks do not certify graphical-client appearance, every character/Mech transition, audible playback, pixel-perfect client pipe seams, GPU performance, multiplayer/desync behavior or a complete balanced campaign. Source-art previews are not game screenshots. See the verification ledger and continuation audit for details.
