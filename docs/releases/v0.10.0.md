## Second Nature 0.10.0: Machine Age

**Stable Factorio 2.0.77 with Space Age, Quality and Elevated Rails. Alpha prerelease.**

Download **`second-nature_0.10.0.zip`**, keep it zipped in the Factorio `mods` folder, and replace older Second Nature copies after backing up saves. Use the named mod ZIP, not GitHub's automatic source archive. A matching SHA-256 file is attached. Private repository downloads require GitHub sign-in.

**Existing saves upgrade in place.** No existing recipe, research cost, power rating, footprint or ecological rate changed in this release. Every one of the 22 pre-existing machines keeps its exact collision box and entity name, so factories, blueprints and saved ecological values carry over untouched. The new content is additive and gated behind its own research.

### Seventeen buildings that complement vanilla instead of replacing it

Most overhaul mods answer "should I keep the vanilla machine?" with a flat no. This release deliberately refuses that. Every building added here trades something real, so the honest answer is "it depends", and a mature factory ends up running stock, clean and dirty machines side by side for different jobs.

**Two furnace families that disagree.** Sealed crucible furnaces, oxygen-blown smelters and an electric arc refinery capture their own flue gas and emit almost nothing (0.2 to 0.5 pollution a minute), but they smelt more slowly per ore and need an oxygen supply. Coke blast furnaces and reverberatory cupolas are cheap, roughly twice as fast on raw ore, and emit 18 to 26 a minute with an explicit toxicity cost the restoration model reads. Smelting dirty while you bootstrap is the correct opening; leaving it that way is what stalls your stages.

Captured flue gas is not free either. Catalytic flue treatment converts it into activated carbon and a poisoned catalyst mesh, which reclamation can recover.

**Ore concentration as a factory project.** A wet ore mill grinds ore into pumpable slurry, a froth flotation bank floats the valuable mineral off and drops the rest as tailings, and a dewatering press squeezes froth into dry concentrate while returning most of the water. Smelted in an oxygen-blown smelter that yields about **2.8 plates per original ore**, against 1.0 for direct sealed smelting and 1.5 for the blast furnace.

It is deliberately not a research unlock that quietly doubles output. It is three separate buildings, a water loop, an oxygen feed, a solid tailings byproduct and roughly 2 MW of draw before the first extra plate appears. Tailings bind back into stone in a kiln, or pile up.

**Mining and research sidegrades.** The electric auger (0.35) and hydraulic mining head (0.5) produce no pollution at all and never out-mine a stock drill at 0.5. The deep core drill is genuinely faster at 2.6 with a wider search radius, and pays with emissions, 3.5 MW and a seven-tile footprint. The field laboratory researches at 1.6x while drawing 320 kW against a stock lab's 60 kW, and accepts every science package the stock lab does.

**Cleaning up after yourself.** Electrostatic smog precipitation removes 180 pollution a cycle and a direct air capture tower removes 520, the strongest cleanup in the mod and thirteen times a scrubber. Both consume real catalysts, clean water and oxygen, and both leave residue to treat. Cleanup is a legitimate strategy. It is never the cheap one.

Three new field guide entries cover the clean and dirty trade, the concentration line and atmospheric cleanup.

### Fixes

**Power pole wires now attach to the pole.** Our field pole uses original art whose crossarm sits at -1.9405 tiles, but the prototype had inherited vanilla's attachment point at -2.578, so every copper, red and green wire in the game floated roughly 0.58 tiles above the insulator it was supposedly bolted to. Attachment points are now derived from the pole mesh itself and regenerate with the art.

### Art

**The explorer is a normal size.** Her body rendered about 1.95 tiles tall, roughly 25 percent oversized next to vanilla characters and machines, which quietly made every building look small. She now stands at 1.58 tiles, inside the stock range, and a regression test prevents the drift from returning.

**Her hairline was rebuilt.** The previous crown was a single flattened ball that began behind the brow, leaving a wide band of bare forehead that read as balding. It is now a continuous lofted scalp shell walked from the hairline over the crown to the nape, with the crown closed by a fan and extra clearance at the widest part of the skull so no scalp shows through.

**Seventeen original procedural building models**, each with a mechanism that reads at a glance: tilting converter vessels, three-electrode arc hearths, rotating ball mills on trunnions, aerated flotation cells with froth launders, filter-plate presses that squeeze, a derrick over a rotary table, and sorbent contactors with induced-draft fans.

**Placeable buildings are marked in the inventory** with a baseplate rule and footing ticks in the icon's bottom margin, so they no longer read as interchangeable with loose materials.

### Audio

The new furnaces, mills, presses, drills and laboratory each get a working sound matched to their mechanism rather than sharing the generic assembler loop. Every referenced file is a stock Factorio asset verified against the pinned 2.0.77 data; nothing is copied into this mod.

### Verification

New checks cover pole wire geometry (copper height, per-rotation signal separation, shadow offsets and generator reproducibility), the industry balance contract (clean recipes must stay slower per unit than dirty ones, green drills must never out-mine stock, cleanup must consume real inputs), save-compatible footprints, fluid port sufficiency, technology reachability, sprite canvas fit, and the explorer's height.
