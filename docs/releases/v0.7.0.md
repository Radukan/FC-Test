## Second Nature 0.7.0: Nightglass

**Stable Factorio 2.0.77 with Space Age, Quality and Elevated Rails. Alpha prerelease.**

Download **`second-nature_0.7.0.zip`**, keep it zipped, and put it in the Factorio `mods` folder. Back up saves and remove older Second Nature copies first. Use the named mod ZIP, not GitHub's automatic source archive. A matching SHA-256 file is attached; private repository downloads require GitHub sign-in.

### Goth explorer, true doubled-resolution art

The adult explorer now has pale skin, a layered black/violet wolf cut, original botanical/circuit tattoos, dark makeup, black protective gear and a short pleated skirt over opaque undershorts. Armor tiers, coordinated movement, native aiming and the right-hand-leading mining grip remain.

The character is freshly rendered at **160 texels per authored unit instead of 80**, with half the sprite scale so she does not become twice as large in the world. Fixed crops and native striped atlas pages keep every input texture within the engine's 8192-pixel limit. This is new high-resolution art, not upscaled old PNGs.

### Three solar-only locomotive tiers

| Locomotive | Fitted roof PV | Battery | Day / night limits |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Sunseed solar shunter | 240 kW | 24 MJ | 91 / 48 km/h |
| Heliograph solar locomotive | 420 kW | 45 MJ | 147 / 91 km/h |
| Daybreak solar express | 600 kW | 72 MJ | 194 / 130 km/h |

Every tier remains slower than a conventional locomotive. Native roof-mounted solar equipment charges the locked onboard battery grid. **No ordinary fuel or factory-grid charging is accepted.** The motor receives only metered joules withdrawn from that battery, with unspent credit reconciled rather than duplicated.

As darkness increases, both the speed ceiling and available motor power are reduced. A depleted battery provides no traction; the train may coast under native physics. Daylight dwell at stations helps recharge. Native rails, stops, wagons and schedules remain in use. Open a solar locomotive's normal GUI for its battery/mode monitor.

### Six power options

- **Copperleaf solar rack:** early passive solar, 18 kW, no direct emissions.
- **Trailblazer burner set:** early chemical-fuel generator, 180 kW, polluting.
- **Helical wind turbine:** passive atmospheric wind, variable output up to 120 kW.
- **Closed-loop biomass engine:** 500 kW from prepared biopellets, no direct pollution; remove and recover mineral ash.
- **Deep-loop geothermal plant:** steady, fuel-free planetary heat, up to 1.5 MW depending on the world.
- **Residue-fired cogenerator:** a 3 MW industrial chemical-fuel generator with substantial real pollution.

The progression includes practical red-science power, midgame wind/biomass, and larger geothermal/industrial options. Fuel generators consume actual fuel and stop when starved or when their residue output blocks. Generating electricity awards no free ecological fitness.
