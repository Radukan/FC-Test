Version: 0.10.0
Date: 2026-09-08
  Features:
    - Add seventeen industrial buildings that complement vanilla rather than replacing it: five furnaces, three assembly cells, a three-stage ore concentration line, three mining heads, two air-cleanup buildings and a field laboratory.
    - Add a full ore multiplication chain: wet milling to slurry, froth flotation to concentrate and tailings, and dewatering back to dry concentrate and reusable process water.
    - Add sealed and oxygen-blown smelting that captures flue gas, plus catalytic flue treatment that turns it into activated carbon.
    - Add coke blast and cupola furnaces: cheap, fast and heavily polluting alternatives that the restoration model fully accounts for.
    - Add electrostatic smog precipitation and direct air capture, the two strongest cleanup operations in the mod.
    - Add a mid-game field laboratory that researches faster and accepts every stock science package.
    - Add three field guide entries covering the clean and dirty industry trade, the concentration line and atmospheric cleanup.
  Fixes:
    - Split the explorer mining animation into pages the engine accepts. The re-render paged it into a ten line stripe against eight directions, which the game rejected outright with "Invalid stripeLine height" before any save could load.
    - Attach power pole wires to the insulators the pole sprite actually draws. Copper previously used the inherited vanilla attachment at -2.578 while our crossarm sits at -1.9405, so every wire floated about 0.58 tiles above the pole.
  Balance:
    - Every clean machine is slower per unit of raw output than its dirty counterpart, so low emissions remain a real trade rather than a free upgrade.
    - Green mining heads mine no faster than a stock drill; the wide-radius deep core drill is fast but emits and occupies seven tiles.
    - Ore concentration costs three separate buildings, real water logistics and oxygen before it pays back more metal per ore.
  Art:
    - Reduce the explorer to a vanilla character height. The body rendered about 1.95 tiles tall, roughly 25 percent oversized next to stock characters and machines; it now stands at 1.58 tiles.
    - Rebuild the explorer hairline as a continuous lofted scalp shell. The previous crown began behind the brow and read as balding, and the crown and nape now close cleanly.
    - Add seventeen original procedural building models with distinct mechanisms: tilting converters, arc electrodes, rotating ball mills, aerated flotation cells, filter presses, derricks and sorbent contactors.
    - Mark placeable buildings in the inventory with a baseplate rule so they read differently from loose materials.
  Audio:
    - Give the new furnaces, mills, presses, drills and laboratory their own working sounds instead of a shared assembler loop.
  Verification:
    - Add pole wire checks covering copper height, per-rotation signal separation, shadow offsets and generator reproducibility.
    - Add industry checks covering save-compatible footprints, the complement-not-replace balance contract, fluid port sufficiency, technology reachability and sprite canvas fit.
    - Add a character height regression test so the explorer cannot silently drift out of the vanilla range again.

Date: 2026-09-06
  Major Features:
    - Initial alpha of the Second Nature Space Age restoration campaign.
    - Five persistent planetary ecosystems, five fitness axes, six stages, toxicity and native resistance.
    - 22 machines, 88 recipes, 20 technologies, 31 materials/science items and six fluids.
    - Three restoration sciences, integrated vanilla progression and a sustained five-world network victory.

    - Enable native pollution layers on all non-spore planets, without introducing arbitrary enemy invasions.
    - Native nest-based response waves with warnings, grace periods, bounded group sizes and peaceful-mode support.
    - Safe local terrain restoration, sparse forests, entity-anchored cryogenic garden overlays.
    - Planetary dashboard, field guide, circuit telemetry, multiplayer force-aware network logistics and read-only remote API.
    - Original inventory icons, research illustrations and environmental branding; tinted built-in machine animations.
  Compatibility:
    - Separate package metadata for Factorio 2.0.77 stable and 2.1.17 experimental.

    - Requires Space Age, Quality and Elevated Rails. Intended for a fresh Space Age freeplay save.

    - Alpha: automated Lua and official source-data tests are provided; real engine and long-form campaign playtesting remain release gates.
