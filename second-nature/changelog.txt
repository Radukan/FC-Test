Version: 0.3.0
Date: 2026-09-07
  Features:
    - Original heavy-industrial sprites and working animations for all 22 restoration/production buildings.
    - Original intact animated expedition lander, permanently protected even after a rocket launch.
    - Original feminine adult explorer with fully covered expedition gear, armor, tool, movement and armed poses.
    - Three tiers of new guns, ammo, turrets, barricades and armor; powered personal shields and field dressings.
    - Four loaded sentries and stronger initial barricades replace the minimal landing perimeter.
    - No early structural wood dependency: mineral stocks, riveted poles/crates, metal gun stocks and algal seeds.
    - Time-integrated habitat recovery and pollution stress. Greening and its reversal take sustained time.
    - Three bounded optimization technologies: +15%, +30%, +45% clean work, capture and supported growth.
  Balance:
    - Baseline ecological work/drift now use 30% of the old rapid coefficients; physical capture uses 25%.
    - Ideal full habitat growth takes about three supported hours; heavy contamination reverses it gradually.
    - Nauvis stage 5 additionally requires at least 80% surveyed habitat condition.
    - Mod-grown trees can wither under prolonged pollution; infrastructure and untracked player trees are preserved.
  Compatibility:
    - Surviving older landers gain permanent protection. Existing cargo and defenses are not duplicated.
    - Character graphics are optional at startup; inventories/controllers and vanilla equipment mechanics remain.
    - Original procedural art is reproducible with the optional art toolchain; no downloaded Wube sprites are bundled.
    - Alpha: graphical, full-campaign and multiplayer validation remain separate from automated engine smoke tests.

Version: 0.2.0
Date: 2026-09-06
  Bugfixes:
    - Namespace all GUI child names: tabs, value, text and state no longer collide with LuaGuiElement members.
    - Validate GUI names against the stable API in runtime tests; protect dashboard opening from fatal GUI errors.
    - Do not call crafting-only recipe APIs on the ecology monitor's constant-combinator entity.
    - Import core utilities explicitly and resolve all modules at control parsing, not from runtime callbacks.
    - Keep the special default map preset valid and explicitly preserve native nest/worm generation.
    - Ignore ungenerated/pollution-only chunks when indexing terrain; chunk removal no longer restarts a survey.
    - Newly detected hotspots immediately block readiness; closing the dashboard cancels pending native confirmation.
  Features:
    - Stable Factorio 2.0 only. Experimental builds are no longer developed or published.
    - Last Landing: one shared cargo lander, two loaded turrets and starter walls per force in new freeplay.
    - Desolate Nauvis without trees/fish, barren ground, legacy smog and three themed map presets.
    - Pollution-fed broods: restoration targets replace pollution-driven biter attack recruitment.
    - Real local pollution suppresses warnings, reduces waves and can send tracked raiders back to their nests.
    - Worldwide pollution inventory, rolling hotspot survey, local smog overlay and two new circuit signals.
    - Dirty local air blocks soil/biodiversity gains. Total pollution and hotspots gate planetary completion.
    - Bounded late-stage Nauvis succession, pollution-damaged grass and reversible water discoloration.
    - Permanent player choice after full Nauvis restoration: friendly Bloombacks and gardens, or eradication.
    - Original eight-direction Bloomback animations, bloom gardens and a Last Landing main-menu illustration.
  Compatibility:
    - Existing saves retain ecology, machines and terrain: no replacement cargo or retroactive sterilization.
    - New landing/map-generation experience requires a fresh save. Back up older saves before upgrading.
    - Alpha: automated validation does not establish a balanced full campaign or graphical/multiplayer certification.

Version: 0.1.0
Date: 2026-09-06
  Major Features:
    - Initial alpha of the Second Nature Space Age restoration campaign.
    - Five persistent planetary ecosystems, five fitness axes, six stages, toxicity and native resistance.
    - 22 machines, 88 recipes, 20 technologies, 31 materials/science items and six fluids.
    - Three restoration sciences, integrated vanilla progression and a sustained five-world network victory.
  Features:
    - Ecology progresses only through completed production cycles, never passive machine ownership.
    - Closed filter, water, thermal-buffer and waste loops; optional dirty industrial forcing.
    - Planet-specific Vulcanus weathering, Fulgoran remediation, Gleban symbiosis and Aquilo gardens.
    - Enable native pollution layers on all non-spore planets, without introducing arbitrary enemy invasions.
    - Native nest-based response waves with warnings, grace periods, bounded group sizes and peaceful-mode support.
    - Safe local terrain restoration, sparse forests, entity-anchored cryogenic garden overlays.
    - Planetary dashboard, field guide, circuit telemetry, multiplayer force-aware network logistics and read-only remote API.
    - Original inventory icons, research illustrations and environmental branding; tinted built-in machine animations.
  Compatibility:
    - Separate package metadata for Factorio 2.0.77 stable and 2.1.17 experimental.
    - Adapts recipe categories and science-pack prototype types to the loaded game branch.
    - Requires Space Age, Quality and Elevated Rails. Intended for a fresh Space Age freeplay save.
  Info:
    - Alpha: automated Lua and official source-data tests are provided; real engine and long-form campaign playtesting remain release gates.
