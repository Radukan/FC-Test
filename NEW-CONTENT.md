# New Content Added — Second Nature Overhaul

## 1. Restoration Nexus (buildings + items + tech)
- **Type:** Assembling machine (3×3 footprint, 500kW, crafting speed 2)
- **Visual:** 256×256 fantastical crystal tower with glowing dome (east + working animation)
- **Function:** Synthesizes ecological materials. Uses `sn-restoration` crafting category.
- **Placeable:** Yes, requires `sn-restoration-nexus` item.
- **Recipe:** 1 arc-refinery, 10 biocrystal cores, 20 mycelial spores, 50 steel plates, 20 circuits, 200 water.
- **Technology:** `sn-restoration-nexus-tech` (500 units, 5 science packs, requires field laboratory).
- **Gameplay impact:** Provides a central synthesis building that visually and mechanically represents the convergence of sealed industry and living ecology. It accelerates restoration work by producing synthesized ecological outputs and serving as a milestone marker.

## 2. New Items
- `sn-biocrystal-core` — crystallized mineral from restored soil. Stack 200. Recipe from algal biomass + stone + water.
- `sn-mycelial-spore` — fungal catalyst. Stack 500. Recipe from bio-ash + wood + water.
- Both unlock with the Nexus technology and feed back into the Nexus recipe.

## 3. Visual Overhaul
- All inventory icons replaced with new fantasy designs (deep steel + emerald + violet + copper).
- Key entities replaced: bloomback, air-scrubber, arc-refinery, field drones, restoration warden armor.
- New building sprite set for Restoration Nexus.
- Achievement/medallion direction documented for future cycles.

## 4. Localization
- English locale entries added for all new entities, items, technologies, and descriptions.

## 5. Design Philosophy
The mod was missing a **central convergence point** — a building that literally embodies the restoration thesis. The Nexus is that point. It is not a faster version of anything existing; it requires new ecological intermediates (crystal + spore) that themselves require new recipes, creating a multi-layer production loop that makes the restoration campaign feel like a genuine industrial project rather than a hidden upgrade tree.
